Privacy Policy

Endless Descends · Effective date: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ycode ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile game Endless Descends ("the game"). By playing the game you agree to this policy.

1. The short version

  • The game has no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your progress (score, coins, wings, settings, unlocked characters) is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads. The companies that provide those ads may collect limited device information, described below.
  • Ads in the game are requested in a child-directed mode, so they are not personalized.

2. Information saved on your device

The game stores your high score, coins, wings, task progress, selected character and sound settings in the game's local storage on your phone. This information is not sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data in your phone settings.

3. Information collected through advertising

The game is free and is supported by advertising. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the advertising services we use may automatically collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (where available)
  • IP address and the approximate location it indicates
  • Device type, operating system version, language and network type
  • Information about how you interact with ads (such as views and clicks)

We do not receive your name, email address or other directly identifying details from these services.

4. Advertising partners

The game uses an advertising mediation service called Unity LevelPlay, which requests ads from advertising networks including Google AdMob and Unity Ads. More networks may be added in the future. These companies act under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (LevelPlay and Unity Ads): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google (AdMob): https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children

The game is suitable for a general audience, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads in the game are requested as child-directed, which means personalized (interest-based) advertising is turned off and ad partners are not allowed to build advertising profiles from play in this game. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has given us personal information, contact us using the details below and we will help.

6. How we use information

We use the limited information described above only to run the game, show ads and keep the game working properly. We do not sell your personal information.

7. Permissions

The game needs internet access and network status access, so that ads can load. It does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

8. Data retention and deletion

We do not keep a database of players. Data saved on your device can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage. For data held by advertising partners, please see their privacy policies linked above.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the game and its data. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this page, and the effective date at the top will change when we do.
